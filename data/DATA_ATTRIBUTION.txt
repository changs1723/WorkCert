Numerical references and software examples retain credit to the source creators.

1. Shahriar Afkhami (2022), Additively manufactured metal honeycombs,
Mendeley Data version 1, DOI 10.17632/8bxkdw4nfw.1.
The 23 CX-steel references use these preparation rules: compression positive,
contact at the first of three consecutive samples at or above 0.5 kN,
chronological record-high displacement, endpoint 0.4 of the nominal
loaded CAD dimension, dense PL interpolation, no smoothing. Twelve Z/TestInput
records and E_Y_0.1 were excluded during preparation. The retained reference
CSVs are included under reference_inputs/.
Associated experiment: Afkhami et al., Thin-Walled Structures 180 (2022), 109864,
DOI 10.1016/j.tws.2022.109864. Descriptor: Data in Brief 46 (2023), 108857,
DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2022.108857.

2. wuqiang, Wu (creator name as deposited, 2025), Supplementary date and code
files for Multi-objective optimization of a bi-material enhanced double-arrow
honeycomb for improving load-bearing capacity and energy absorption,
Zenodo version 1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16399749.
The EDAH4_52_35(3.3).csv force-travel record is used in its entirety: 7817 signed
force observations, force converted from N to kN, initial travel subtracted.
The prepared source CSV is retained under reference_inputs/ without modification.

3. Ahmed Saber (2026), Quasi-static compression test dataset of
sinusoidally-corrugated multi-cell PLA+ energy absorbers fabricated by fused
deposition modeling (FDM), Mendeley Data version 4, DOI 10.17632/v7p6y654ft.4.
Forty design records (01--40), three nominal SCMC records and three straight-wall
tube records are used. Each source workbook contains an archived 1000-point
curve explicitly labelled Displacement (mm), Force (kN). All 55 XLSX files were
downloaded and verified against the repository SHA256 values. Nine auxiliary
material/geometry/indicator workbooks are retained but excluded from compression
curve counts. Photographs and STL files are outside this numerical subset.
All signed force values are retained. The entire available loading window is
used; no smoothing, clipping, contact threshold or resampling is introduced.
Related article as linked by the repository: DOI 10.1515/mt-2026-0038.

The PLA+ inputs are archived 1000-point tabulations. Source and prepared-reference
hashes, units, node counts and eligibility checks are in metadata/reference_audit.json.
All error certificates concern the corresponding PL numerical references.
Original steel and EDAH acquisition files and official repository metadata are
included byte-for-byte under source_context/. Its manifest links these sources
to the fixed reference inputs and records the preparation rules and exclusions.
The original experiments and data remain credited to the source contributors.
