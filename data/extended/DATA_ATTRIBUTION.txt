The original XLSX, official repository metadata and download checksum are retained
under cyclic_RHS_10829958/. The repository MD5 is independently matched, and a
SHA-256 is recorded. The workbook describes austenitic stainless steel RHS specimen
S1-L1, section 120 x 80 x 6 mm, effective length 1,650 mm, in major-axis cyclic
bending. Horizontal displacement was measured by a string potentiometer and
horizontal force by the actuator. The workbook explicitly labels mm and kN.

Transformation: numerical rows from worksheet S1_L1, columns B (displacement)
and C (force), starting at row 4, are extracted in their original order. There
are 125,157 finite numerical pairs. No scaling, force-sign change, smoothing,
contact threshold, reversal threshold, or loading-envelope extraction is applied.
The original sheet has two labelled header rows and an initial blank row.
The optional derived cyclic_RHS_raw.csv records the same numerical values in
17-digit text form; validation reconstructs them from the original XLSX.

Chronological direction, exact reversals and equal-displacement force changes
are preserved through branch/event metadata. The resulting signed F dx total
is not interpreted as closed-cycle dissipation. The original experiments and
data remain credited to the source authors.

PLA+ masses used by the separate exchange example are original data from
Ahmed Saber (2026), Mendeley Data version 4, DOI 10.17632/v7p6y654ft.4,
CC BY 4.0. The original Mass_and_Crash_Indicators.xlsx is already retained
under ../raw/pla_v7p6y654ft_v4/. Numeric m(g) values are matched by specimen ID
to all 46 force curves. Derived indicators are recomputed from the archived
force histories at the explicitly requested travel limits. The source-reported
delta_max convention is retained in a separate source audit.
