Reproduction and timing scope
The tested locked benchmark environment is Python3.12.14/NumPy2.3.5/
Numba0.68.1; it does not change the package's NumPy-only runtime requirements.
Numba compiles candidate-edge and DP loops, not WorkCert's public implementation.
Candidate-edge computation is O(n^3); DP is O(K n^2), memory O(n^2+K n).
Per-case construction samples are descriptive and are not used for speedup
claims. The separate controlled scaling experiment evaluates implementation
cost. All local dependency environments and JIT caches must be excluded from
public source and submission archives.
